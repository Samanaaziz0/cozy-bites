Our Story

Passion Plated,
Halal at Heart

Founded in 2012 by Chef Marco and his business partner Ali Raza, Cozy Bites began as a small 12-table restaurant with a singular mission: serve honest, soul-warming food that honours both Western culinary tradition and the rich flavours of Pakistan — all strictly Halal.

Over a decade later, we've grown — but never changed what matters. Every dish is 100% Halal certified, and our kitchen proudly serves both classic continental and authentic Pakistani cuisine.
